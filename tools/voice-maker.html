Voice Maker

Your words, spoken. Paste a script or load it from a file, pick a voice, and get downloadable WAV audio — long scripts are voiced part by part, each with its own player.

Step 1 — The script

Step 2 — The voice

Honest limit: free Gemini text-to-speech has a small daily cap per key — roughly 15 generations a day is the figure commonly published (AlphaPost publishes the same). If parts start failing with the daily-limit message, add a second key in Settings or continue tomorrow.

How this tool works

1
Paste your script, or load it from a .txt (instant) or a PDF (Gemini reads the text out first).
2
Pick one of the 30 voices and, if you want, a style instruction like "calm and slow".
3
Tap Make the voice — long scripts split at sentence boundaries and are voiced part by part; each part shows its own status, player and download.
4
Download the WAV parts and join them in any editor — or use them as separate scene voices.

What you need

  • Your free Gemini key added in Settings (one minute, free)
  • A script — typed, pasted, or in a .txt / PDF file

What you get

  • Downloadable WAV audio for every part of your script
  • A live per-part status list for long scripts
  • Your last script restored into the box when you return

Tips

  • One voice per project keeps a channel recognisable — note which voice you used.
  • Short paragraphs in the script voice more naturally than one wall of text.
  • If the daily voice cap hits mid-script, the finished parts stay — only the failed parts need a rerun.

FAQ

Why parts instead of one file?

The voice model accepts a few thousand characters per call. Splitting at sentences keeps quality — and a failed part never takes the finished parts down with it.

Is there an unlimited on-device voice?

No — this site has no on-device voice engine, and it will not pretend to. The voices are Gemini's, on your free key, with its daily cap stated above.

Can it read Urdu / Hindi?

Yes — write the script in the language and the voice reads it. Roman Urdu reads most reliably.
