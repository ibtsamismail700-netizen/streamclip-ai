AI Answer Checker

Search is becoming answers. This checker scores your page on the signals that decide whether ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity can quote it — and tells you the exact fix for every miss. The score itself is free: no key needed.

Step 1 — The page

Or paste the page text

If a site blocks fetching, copy the page's text (or its HTML) and paste it here. The wording checks still run; title/meta/schema checks will show as missing, because pasted text does not carry them.

How this tool works

1
Paste any public page link and tap Check this page.
2
The checker fetches the page and runs ten deterministic checks: title, meta description, single H1, question-style headings, an early direct answer, FAQ schema, any JSON-LD, list structure, llms.txt, and AI crawlers in robots.txt.
3
You get a score /100 with a pass or the exact fix for every signal.
4
Optionally, with your Gemini key added, tap Get AI fix suggestions — ordered fixes, a rewritten opening, and a ready-to-paste FAQ schema written only from your page's own content.

What you need

  • Nothing for the score — it is deterministic and free
  • Your free Gemini key added in Settings (one minute, free) (only for the optional AI suggestions)

What you get

  • An AI Answer score /100 with per-check pass/fail
  • The exact fix for every failing check
  • Optional: AI fix list, rewritten intro, and a copyable FAQPage JSON-LD block

Tips

  • Fix in the order shown — title, early answer and question headings move the score most.
  • Check your top 3 pages, not all of them; the same fixes usually repeat.
  • Re-check after fixing — the score is deterministic, so improvements show immediately.

FAQ

Does a high score guarantee AI engines quote me?

No — it means your page is quotable: readable, structured and allowed. Whether an engine quotes it also depends on authority and competition, which no checker can promise.

Why did fetching fail?

Some sites block readers or sit behind logins. The paste mode is the honest fallback — the wording checks still run.

What is llms.txt?

A small plain-text file at your site's root that maps your content for AI readers. Most sites do not have one yet — which is exactly why it is an easy win.
