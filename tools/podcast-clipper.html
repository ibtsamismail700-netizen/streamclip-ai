How this tool works

1
Paste the YouTube link of a podcast or interview episode.
2
Choose the clip length, how many clips to pre-pick, what to weight the scoring toward, and the minimum score.
3
Tap Find podcast moments — Gemini listens in 10-minute windows and scores every moment 0–99.
4
Check any moment with its Watch this scene link (it opens YouTube at that exact second), adjust the picks, then make the clips plan.
5
Download or copy the plan — each clip carries its time, hook and reason. The final videos are made from it in the render step.

What you need

  • Your free Gemini key added in Settings (one minute, free)
  • A YouTube podcast or interview link (public videos work best)

What you get

  • Best-moments cards with strict 0–99 scores
  • A Watch-this-scene link for every moment, so each scene is provably from that episode
  • A downloadable clips plan with hooks and reasons

Tips

  • Long, lively episodes score best — flat small-talk segments are skipped by design.
  • Lower the minimum score to 60 on quiet, thoughtful podcasts.
  • A moment that starts as the OTHER speaker begins a story usually holds viewers best — the scorer notes these in "why".

FAQ

Why conversation tuning?

A gameplay scanner hunts explosions; a podcast scanner hunts talk — laughter, insights, disagreements and quotable lines, and it listens for speaker changes because a fresh voice starting is where clips naturally begin.

Does it make the video files?

Not on this page. Like the Stream Clipper, it produces the verified plan (times, hooks, scores); the downloadable videos are made from that plan in the render step, and the page says so plainly rather than faking a file.

The scan stopped partway — why?

Private, age-restricted or extremely long videos can fail on some windows. Windows that fail are reported in the steps and the rest still rank.
