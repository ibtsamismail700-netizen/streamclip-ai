Voice Changer Dub

Record your line, pick a character, and hear it spoken back in that character's voice — for skits, comedy videos and dubs.

How this honestly works.
No AI can yet filter your real voice into another voice. So this tool does the real thing in two steps: it reads your line (transcribes your recording), then an AI character voice speaks that line. Your recording itself is never altered or uploaded anywhere except to Google for the transcription, with your key.

Step 1 — Your line

Record a short line (a sentence or two is perfect), or upload a short clip — up to ~18 MB.

Step 2 — Pick the character

How this tool works

1
Record your line with the mic button, or upload a short audio/video clip.
2
Tap Read my line — Gemini transcribes the words. Check them in the box and fix any mistake (you can also just type a line).
3
Pick the character — old man, child-like, villain, news anchor, woman or man.
4
Tap Re-voice my line and play or download the result as a .wav for your video.

What you need

  • Your free Gemini key added in Settings (one minute, free)
  • A microphone, or a short recording of the line (up to ~18 MB)

What you get

  • Your line transcribed to text
  • The line spoken by an AI character voice — playable + downloadable .wav

Tips

  • Short lines land best — one or two sentences per character voice.
  • For comedy skits, record each character's lines separately and cut them together in your editor.
  • If the transcription misses a word, just type the correction — the voice reads the box, not the recording.

FAQ

Is this my own voice, changed?

No — and the page says so openly. It is an AI voice speaking your words. A true filter that keeps your performance and changes only the voice is not something Gemini offers.

Which languages work?

The transcription and voices handle Urdu, Hindi and English lines. Mixed lines are read as written.

The mic button does nothing.

Allow microphone access when the browser asks — or use the upload button instead.
