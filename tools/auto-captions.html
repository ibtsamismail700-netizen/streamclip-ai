Auto Captions

Captions from the video itself — no typing, no transcript hunting. Upload, choose a style, download the .srt.

Step 1 — Your video

Step 2 — Caption options

How this tool works

1
Upload your video file (up to ~18 MB).
2
Choose the caption style, the spoken language (or Auto), and an optional translation language.
3
Tap Make my captions — Gemini listens to the whole video and transcribes it into timed segments.
4
Preview the captions on the page, then download the .srt or .vtt — plus the translated pair if you asked for one.

What you need

  • Your free Gemini key added in Settings (one minute, free)
  • A video file with speech in it, up to ~18 MB

What you get

  • A styled .srt and .vtt, ready for YouTube, TikTok editors and players
  • An optional second-language caption pair
  • A full on-page preview before you download

Tips

  • Timing is segment-level: one cue per spoken segment of a few seconds. That is the honest baseline of AI transcription — check names and numbers, which can be misheard.
  • Short-lines style is best for vertical videos; karaoke suits lyric and word-by-word edits.
  • For a YouTube video instead of a file, the Repurpose Pack generates captions from the link.

FAQ

How accurate is the timing?

Segment timing is honest — each cue covers the seconds that speech was heard. The karaoke style divides a segment's time evenly across its words; it is labelled an estimate because the model does not return measured per-word timing.

Why the 18 MB limit?

The file is sent to Gemini directly from your browser as one request; ~18 MB keeps that reliable. Trim longer videos to the part you need captioned.

SRT or VTT — which do I need?

SRT works almost everywhere (YouTube uploads, most editors). VTT is the web-player standard. Both are included so you never convert by hand.
