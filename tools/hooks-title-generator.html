An example pack

For the topic "I beat the hardest boss in the game with no armour — 57 tries", a pack looks like this (example):

Hooks
  1. No armour. 57 tries. One boss.
  2. Everyone said this was impossible
  3. I refused to wear armour…
Titles
  1. I Beat the Hardest Boss With NO Armour (57 Tries)
  2. No Armour vs The Final Boss — Full Fight
Hashtags

#gaming #bossfight #challenge #shorts

Tips

  • A topic with a number or a result ("57 tries") beats a vague topic.
  • Use the hook as on-screen text in the first 2 seconds of the video.
  • Generate twice and mix the best lines from both rounds.
