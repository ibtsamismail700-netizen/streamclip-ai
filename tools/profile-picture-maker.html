How this tool works

1
Upload a clear, front-facing photo — or switch to Describe and write the avatar you want.
2
Pick a style, how many versions, the background, and a frame ring.
3
Tap Create my pictures — each version is generated by the image model; with a photo, the model edits YOUR picture, keeping you recognisable.
4
Tap the version you like to pick it. Change the ring any time — every version re-frames instantly on canvas.
5
Download your pick in exact sizes: Instagram/TikTok 1080×1080, YouTube 800×800, Facebook 720×720, WhatsApp 640×640.

What you need

  • Your free Gemini key added in Settings (one minute, free)
  • A clear front-facing photo of yourself — or a two-sentence description

What you get

  • 2 or 4 AI-styled versions to pick from
  • 5 frame rings, applied on canvas after generation
  • Square 1:1 masters with exact platform-size downloads

Tips

  • A clear, well-lit selfie gives a recognisable result; group photos and sunglasses confuse the model.
  • Image generation uses your free image allowance — 2 versions is the economical run while you test a style.
  • Professional Headshot + Blurred background is the safe combo for LinkedIn and WhatsApp.

FAQ

Does it really use my photo?

Yes — your photo is sent to the image model as an input and the results are AI-styled versions of it. The "Keep-me-but-better" style changes the least: clean background and studio light, still clearly you.

Why is the ring separate from the AI?

So you can change your mind for free. The AI makes the portrait; the ring is drawn on canvas afterwards, and switching rings never spends your image allowance.

Are the downloads really the stated sizes?

Yes — every size is rendered fresh on canvas from the full-quality master at exactly those pixels, frame included.
