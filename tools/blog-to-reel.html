Blog-to-Reel

Your article already holds the answer — the reel's job is to make people need it. This builds the teaser: hook, rising curiosity, and a full stop right before the answer.

How this tool works

1
Paste the article (and its title, for the pack name).
2
Pick the teaser format — Unfinished story, Countdown, Quiz or Facts — plus the language and voice.
3
Tap Make my teaser pack — the script is written to rise step by step and stop before the answer; the pack shows you exactly what is being withheld.
4
Download the voice and captions, post the reel yourself, and pin the first comment with your article link.

What you need

  • Your free Gemini key added in Settings (one minute, free)
  • A finished article to paste (a few paragraphs at minimum)

What you get

  • A teaser script (hook + 5–8 lines) that stops before the answer
  • The AI voice track (.wav)
  • An end-card line: "Full answer on the blog"
  • The first-comment text and posting caption
  • Estimated-timing captions (.srt) and the full pack (.md)
  • Optional: one AI picture per line

Tips

  • Pin the first comment yourself the moment the reel posts — an unpinned link comment sinks.
  • The withheld point should be the article's best one, not a detail — curiosity is the whole budget.
  • Caption timings are estimated from word counts; nudge them in your editor if a line runs long.

FAQ

Does it post the reel or the comment?

Never — StreamClip does not touch your accounts. You post the reel and pin the comment; the pack just makes both ready.

What stops the teaser from clickbait?

The script is built only from your article, and the pack names the withheld answer openly — if the article does not deliver it, do not post the teaser.

Can I use it for a video instead of an article?

Paste the video's description or transcript and point the end card at the video — the mechanics are the same.
