How this page works

Ask your lecture: type any question about the material. Answers come only from your lecture/PDF — never from general knowledge dressed up as your teacher's words.

What you need

  • Your free Gemini key added in Settings (one minute, free)
  • A YouTube lecture, a PDF (up to ~18 MB), or your study text

What you get

  • A practice paper with a revealable answer key
  • A one-page revision sheet in 6 designs
  • A tutor that answers only from your material

Tips

  • Cover the answer key with your hand and answer out loud first — reveal only after.
  • Regenerate at Hard a few days before the exam; the traps repeat in real papers.
  • Ask "why" and "how" questions — they expose the gaps MCQs hide.

FAQ

Are the answers guaranteed correct?

They are generated strictly from your material, and the model answers follow it closely — but always trust your teacher's notes over any AI output in a conflict.

Why did the tutor say "not covered"?

Because your lecture/document genuinely does not cover that question. That honesty is the point — it never invents an answer.
