News Card Maker

The card every news page runs on: a bold TV-style breaking-news graphic with your headline and channel name. The card itself needs no key — it is drawn on your device.

Step 1 — The news

Card size

The card redraws live as you type. Only post news you have verified — a card makes a claim look official, so the facts are your responsibility.

How this tool works

1
Write the headline and the confirmed details, your channel name, and pick the category and language.
2
The card draws itself live — adjust the words until it reads like television.
3
Tap Download the card for the PNG, ready to post.
4
Optionally tap Improve with AI for three headline variants (same facts, different angles) and a posting caption — tap "Use this" to put a variant straight onto the card.

What you need

  • Nothing for the card itself — it is drawn in your browser
  • Your free Gemini key added in Settings (one minute, free) (only for the AI variants + caption)

What you get

  • A TV-style news card PNG (1080×1080 or 1200×628)
  • Three AI headline variants + a posting caption

Tips

  • Short headlines win: if it does not fit in two breaths, it will not be read on a phone.
  • The details lines are where trust is built — who, where, when, and nothing unconfirmed.
  • Keep one channel name and stick to it — the bottom bar is your brand.

FAQ

Does the card need my Gemini key?

No. The card is drawn on canvas in your browser. The key is only used if you ask for AI headline variants and a caption.

The Urdu text looks broken on the card.

Canvas uses your device's fonts — most Android phones render Urdu correctly. If yours does not, the English card always works.

Can I add a photo to the card?

Not in this version — the clean text card is the format TV channels and big pages use for text news.
