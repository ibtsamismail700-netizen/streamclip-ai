Your Own Branding

Save your own name and logo once, preview them on your video, and export branded frames — your brand on every clip, nobody else's.

Your brand

Saved in your browser only. It is applied to your previews and exports below — never uploaded anywhere.

How it sits on the video

The frame button grabs the current frame with your branding burned in — perfect for thumbnails. The video export renders your logo and name onto every frame of the video, in your browser, at your chosen corner, size and opacity.

How this tool works

1
Type your channel name and upload your logo — both are saved in your browser.
2
Choose the logo corner, size and opacity — the preview updates live.
3
Upload any of your videos: your name and logo appear on the preview exactly as they will be exported.
4
Grab a branded frame (PNG) for thumbnails, or export the full branded video (WebM) with the overlay rendered onto every frame.
5
Use the same name, logo and corner on every clip so viewers start recognising you.

What you need

  • Your logo file and channel name (saved once, in your browser)
  • A video to brand

What you get

  • A reusable brand kit stored in your browser
  • Branded frame exports (PNG) for thumbnails and posts
  • Full branded video exports (WebM) with your overlay burned into every frame

Tips

  • A transparent PNG logo looks professional on any video.
  • Keep the name short — it must be readable at phone size.
  • Use one consistent logo position across all your videos.

FAQ

Is my logo uploaded to a server?

No. It is stored in your browser's local storage and drawn onto frames on your device.

Does it brand the full video?

Yes — "Export branded video" renders your logo and name onto every frame in your browser and downloads a WebM. It renders in real time, so a 5-minute video takes about 5 minutes; keep the tab open while it works.
