Your Own Branding

Save your own name and logo once, preview them on your video, and export branded frames — your brand on every clip, nobody else's.

Your brand

Saved in your browser only. It is applied to your previews below — never uploaded anywhere.

Play the video to any frame, then download it branded — perfect for thumbnails and posts. For full branded video exports, apply the same logo in your editor; this page handles the brand kit and frame exports.

How this tool works

1
Type your channel name and upload your logo — both are saved in your browser.
2
Upload any of your videos: your name appears bottom-left and your logo bottom-right, live on the preview.
3
Play to a strong frame and tap the download button to export that frame with your branding burned in.
4
Use the same name and logo on every clip so viewers start recognising you.

What you need

  • Your logo file and channel name (saved once, in your browser)
  • A video to preview

What you get

  • A reusable brand kit stored in your browser
  • Branded frame exports (PNG) for thumbnails and posts

Tips

  • A transparent PNG logo looks professional on any video.
  • Keep the name short — it must be readable at phone size.
  • Use one consistent logo position across all your videos.

FAQ

Is my logo uploaded to a server?

No. It is stored in your browser's local storage and drawn onto frames on your device.

Does it brand the full video?

This page brands frame exports and shows the live overlay preview. Full-length branded rendering needs a video engine; apply the same overlay in the Upload tool's export flow or your editor.
