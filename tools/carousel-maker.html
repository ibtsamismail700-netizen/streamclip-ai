How this tool works

1
Write the topic — or paste a story/article and let it be re-cut into slides.
2
Set the slide count, size, theme and language; add your handle and, if you like, your own photos.
3
Tap Make my carousel — slide copy is written first (hook slide, one idea per slide, CTA last), then every slide is designed.
4
Download the slides one by one or all together, copy the caption, and post the carousel yourself in order.

What you need

  • Your free Gemini key added in Settings (one minute, free)
  • A topic or a pasted story — photos optional

What you get

  • 5–10 designed slide PNGs at exact Instagram pixels (1080×1350 or 1080×1080)
  • Slide copy with a hook first slide and a CTA last slide
  • The full posting caption with hashtags

Tips

  • Portrait (1080×1350) takes more feed space than square — it is the default for a reason.
  • One idea per slide is the whole game; if a slide needs a second sentence of small print, split it.
  • Change the theme or add photos after generating — the slides redraw instantly without spending another AI call.

FAQ

Does it post the carousel to Instagram?

No — StreamClip never posts to your accounts. You download the slides and post them; that is the no-accounts rule.

Are the slide pictures AI images?

Only if you skip photos and a theme needs none — by default slides are clean designed graphics (colour, type, your handle). Your own photos are used exactly as uploaded.

The text overflows a slide.

Long pasted stories can produce long slides; shorten the source text or raise the slide count and run again.
