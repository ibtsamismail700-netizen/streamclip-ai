Podcast-to-Posts

One recording, a week of posts. Gemini listens to the episode, collects its real quotable lines, and builds the pack: quote cards, a summary post, a thread and the top clip moments.

Step 1 — The episode

How this tool works

1
Paste the episode link and pick the post language.
2
Tap Make my post pack — Gemini listens in 10-minute windows, collecting real quotes, the main points, and clippable moments.
3
The posts are then composed only from what was collected — the summary and thread arrange the episode's own material, nothing invented.
4
Download the 3 quote cards, copy the summary post and the thread, and follow the Watch links to cut the top moments with the Podcast Clipper.

What you need

  • Your free Gemini key added in Settings (one minute, free)
  • A YouTube podcast or interview link

What you get

  • 3 quote cards (1080×1080 PNG) with the speaker and show name
  • A summary post with hashtags
  • A numbered 5-post thread, each post under 280 characters
  • The top 3 clip moments with Watch-this-scene links

Tips

  • Post the quote cards on the days between episodes — they keep the show warm.
  • The thread's first post is the hook; if it does not travel, the rest will not be read.
  • Quote cards keep the original words — if a quote reads awkwardly, that is how it was said; pick another from a longer scan.

FAQ

Are the quotes exact?

They are collected by Gemini from the audio and written into the cards as returned. For a public figure or a sponsor post, verify a quote with the Watch link habit — listen before you publish.

Why only the first hour?

A post pack needs the strongest material, not the whole archive — and an hour keeps the free-key cost sane. The Podcast Clipper scans up to 4 hours when you need clips.

Can it post these for me?

No — StreamClip never posts to your accounts. You download and post; that is the no-accounts rule that keeps the site free of signups.
