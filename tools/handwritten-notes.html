How this tool works

1
Add your source — a YouTube lecture link, a PDF, or pasted text — and tap Analyse once. An already-analysed lecture arrives here loaded.
2
Choose the ink colour, the paper and the handwriting, plus length and language.
3
Tap Write my handwritten notes — the notes are generated in the lecture's own order and inked onto the sheet.
4
Download the sheet as a picture (PNG), print it, or save the plain notes file (.md).
5
Change ink, paper or handwriting any time after — the same notes re-ink instantly.

What you need

  • Your free Gemini key added in Settings (one minute, free)
  • A YouTube lecture, a PDF (up to ~18 MB), or your study text

What you get

  • A handwriting-style notes sheet (title, numbered sections, key ideas, formulas, Remember strip)
  • 4 ink colours × 3 papers × 2 handwritings
  • PNG picture download, print, and the plain .md notes

Tips

  • The content is identical in honesty to Lecture to Notes — only the look changes. Facts are never decorated into being wrong.
  • Blue ink on ruled paper is the classic notebook look; red ink is best kept for the sheets you share.
  • Share the PNG in your class group — every shared sheet carries the StreamClip AI line at the bottom.

FAQ

Is it real handwriting?

It is a handwriting-style font rendering of your real notes — clean, readable and printable, not a scanned page. The words are generated from your lecture exactly like Lecture to Notes.

Why two handwritings?

A neat flowing hand (Caveat) and a rounder, bolder one (Kalam). Pick the one that looks like your notebook.

Do Urdu and Hindi notes work?

Yes — the notes are generated in the chosen language; the handwriting fonts cover Latin script best, so Urdu/Hindi sheets render in the browser's handwriting fallback where needed.
