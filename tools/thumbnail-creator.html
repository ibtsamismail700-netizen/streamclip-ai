How this tool works

1
Creator tab: describe the video, write the headline, pick an art style and text colour.
2
In the bottom bar, tap + and pick the aspect ratio — 16:9 for YouTube, 9:16 for Shorts. The output file is made at that ratio's exact pixel size.
3
Tap "Create thumbnail" — the AI paints the background and your headline is composed on top, ready to download.
4
Redesigner tab: upload a thumbnail and tap "Analyse" — the AI reads its topic, exact text, subject, strengths and weaknesses.
5
Tap "Generate different versions" — you get 6 completely different designs (Bold Yellow, Dark Neon, Red Alert, Clean Mint, Purple Pop, Mono Contrast) at your chosen ratio, all keeping the SAME text.

What you need

  • Your free Gemini key added in Settings (one minute, free)
  • For redesigns: a thumbnail image
  • The exact text for the new versions (analysis fills it in for you)

What you get

  • A designer-level analysis of your thumbnail
  • 6 ready-to-download redesigns that look completely different, same text
  • AI-created thumbnails from a prompt, at 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:3 or 3:4 exact sizes

Tips

  • Keep text to 3–5 words — it must be readable at phone size.
  • One face, one emotion, one idea per thumbnail wins clicks.
  • Download two versions and A/B test them on YouTube.

FAQ

Do the new versions keep my text exactly?

Yes — the version maker draws the exact text you typed on every version. Only the design changes.

Can the prompt box really generate an image?

Yes, when your Gemini key has access to Google's image model, the image is generated and shown here. If not, the AI returns a detailed concept you can build with the version maker.
