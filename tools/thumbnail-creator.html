How this tool works

1
Creator tab: describe the video, write the headline, pick an art style and text colour.
2
In the bottom bar, tap + and pick the output size by platform name — YouTube Thumbnail, Shorts / Reel / Story, Instagram Post, Facebook Ad and more. The output file is made at that size's exact pixels.
3
Tap "Create thumbnail" — a designer brief is written for your choices, the AI paints the artwork to it, and your headline is composed on top in your text style, ready to download.
4
Redesigner tab: upload a thumbnail and tap "Analyse" — the AI reads its topic, exact text, subject, strengths and weaknesses.
5
Pick your design options and tap "Generate different versions" — you get up to 6 completely different designs (Bold Yellow, Dark Neon, Red Alert, Clean Mint, Purple Pop, Mono Contrast) at your chosen size, all keeping the SAME text.

Before / after — what a redesign does

Before (described)

A busy gameplay screenshot with small white text in the corner: "I SURVIVED 100 DAYS". The face is tiny, three other characters crowd the frame, and at phone size the text disappears.

After (what the Redesigner makes)

The same words — "I SURVIVED 100 DAYS" — huge and bold across the design, one shocked face close-up, a single dramatic background, nothing else competing. Six versions of that idea, in different styles, at the exact size you picked.

Tips

  • Keep text to 3–5 words — it must be readable at phone size.
  • One face, one emotion, one idea per thumbnail wins clicks.
  • Download two versions and A/B test them on YouTube.

FAQ

Do the new versions keep my text exactly?

Yes — every version is generated with the exact text from the text box. Only the design changes.

What if the image model cannot answer?

The card says so openly, with Google's real message, and that version falls back to a designed background with your headline text — you still get a usable thumbnail, and the card tells you which kind it is.
