Ramadan / Eid Content Kit

The seasonal pack pages run on: seven ready dua cards, a spoken reminder for sehri/iftar (or Eid / Jumma), and a greeting card carrying your name.

Step 1 — Pick the pack

How this tool works

1
Pick the pack — Ramadan daily duas, Eid, or Jumma (Friday).
2
Choose the language for translations and the reminder script, add your name for the greeting card, and pick a voice.
3
Tap Make my kit — seven dua cards are designed, the reminder script is written and voiced, and the greeting card is drawn.
4
Verify the duas before posting — the kit says so on its own result, and it matters: check each Arabic text against a trusted source.

What you need

  • Your free Gemini key added in Settings (one minute, free)
  • Your name, if you want it on the greeting card

What you get

  • 7 dua cards (1080×1350 PNG) with Arabic + translation
  • A reminder script (sehri/iftar, Eid morning, or Friday) + its AI voice (.wav)
  • A greeting card (1080×1080) with your name

Tips

  • Post one dua card a day — a series trains your audience to return daily.
  • The reminder voice over a quiet nasheed-free background (or plain) keeps the post halal-audio friendly.
  • Eid kits made in the last ten days of Ramadan get shared the most.

FAQ

Are the duas authentic?

The writer is instructed to use only short, widely-known duas and never to compose new Arabic. Even so, they are AI-drafted: verify every card against a trusted Quran/hadith source before posting. Religious text must be exact.

Can I change the name on the greeting card?

Yes — change the name field and run again; the card is redrawn each run.

Is this only for Ramadan?

No — the Eid and Jumma packs work all year. Jumma cards are an every-Friday habit for many pages.
