Viral Hook Translator

Viral hooks follow structures. This tool watches a viral video, extracts the exact structure of its first seconds — then pours your topic into that same structure, in your language.

Step 1 — The viral video

Step 2 — Your topic, same structure

How this tool works

1
Paste a viral video's link and tap Analyse the hook — Gemini watches its opening and writes down the hook's structure: the pattern name, what happens in the first seconds, the on-screen text, and the promise it makes.
2
Read the structure card — it is the reusable lesson, yours to keep even without this tool.
3
Type your topic, pick the hook language, and tap Write my hooks.
4
You get 5 hooks that follow the same structure beat for beat, each with a note on which step of the structure it performs. Copy the one that fits and say it in your first 2 seconds.

What you need

  • Your free Gemini key added in Settings (one minute, free)
  • A YouTube link to a viral video in any niche
  • Your own topic, in a sentence

What you get

  • The viral hook's structure, named and explained
  • 5 fresh hooks for your topic in Urdu, Hindi, Roman or English
  • A per-hook note showing how the structure is being followed

Tips

  • Pick viral videos from YOUR niche — a gaming structure poured onto a cooking topic still works, but a same-niche structure fits best.
  • Analyse 3–4 viral videos and compare structures; the pattern that repeats is the one your niche rewards.
  • The hook is a promise — only use a structure whose promise your video actually keeps.

FAQ

Is this copying the viral video?

No. Ideas and structures are not the video. Your hooks are written fresh for your topic and never reuse the original's words, footage or details — the tool is instructed to never even mention the source video.

Why does the language matter?

A hook translated word-for-word sounds dead. These hooks are written natively in the language you pick, in spoken style.

The analysis failed on my link.

Private, age-restricted or very short videos can fail. Public videos work best.
