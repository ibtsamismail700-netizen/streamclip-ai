How this tool works

1
Add your source — a YouTube lecture link, a PDF, or pasted text — and tap Analyse once. If you already analysed this lecture in another student tool, it arrives here loaded.
2
Choose the number of questions, the types, the difficulty and the time limit.
3
Tap Set my mock test and answer the paper on the page — tap an option for each MCQ, write short answers in the boxes.
4
Tap Mark my test (or let the timer end). MCQs are marked exactly with the reason shown; short questions reveal model answers for honest self-marking.
5
Your result shows the score and a Revise these topics list built from your wrong answers — jump straight into Lecture to Notes to fix them.

What you need

  • Your free Gemini key added in Settings (one minute, free)
  • A YouTube lecture, a PDF (up to ~18 MB), or your study text

What you get

  • A timed practice paper of 10–50 questions from your own material
  • Exact MCQ marking with reasons, and model answers for short questions
  • A score out of 100 and a personal revise list

Tips

  • Use the timer — untimed practice feels easier than the real hall.
  • Short answers are self-marked against the model answer on purpose: no AI pretends to grade written answers fairly.
  • Regenerate at Hard in the last week; the traps repeat in real papers.

FAQ

Are the questions from my lecture only?

Yes — the paper is set strictly from your analysed material, in its own topic order. Nothing is pulled from general knowledge.

Why are short questions not auto-marked?

Honesty: written answers deserve a human judgement. You get the full model answer and mark yourself point by point — the way good teachers train students to self-check.

Can I retake it?

Any time — regenerate for a fresh paper from the same analysed lecture, no re-analysing needed.
