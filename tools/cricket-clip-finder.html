How this tool works

1
Paste the YouTube link of a match or highlights video.
2
Choose the clip length, how many to pre-pick, and the minimum score.
3
Tap Find cricket moments — Gemini watches in 10-minute windows and tags every moment: Wicket, Six, Four, Catch, Funny or Moment.
4
Filter the list by type, check any moment with its Watch this scene link, adjust the picks, then make the clips plan.

What you need

  • Your free Gemini key added in Settings (one minute, free)
  • A YouTube cricket match or highlights link (public videos work best)

What you get

  • Best-moments cards tagged by type, with strict 0–99 scores
  • A Watch-this-scene link for every moment
  • A downloadable clips plan with hooks and reasons

Tips

  • Highlights videos score cleaner than full replays — the dead overs are already gone.
  • Funny moments (crowd, celebrations, commentary gold) often out-travel wickets on new pages.
  • Post the best moment while the match is still being talked about — cricket clips age in hours.

FAQ

Does it make the video files?

Not on this page. It produces the verified plan (times, types, hooks, scores); the downloadable videos are made from that plan in the render step.

A wicket was tagged as a Catch.

A great catch that takes a wicket can be either — the tag is the AI's best call; the Watch link lets you verify in one tap.

The scan stopped partway — why?

Private, age-restricted or extremely long videos can fail on some windows. Windows that fail are reported in the steps and the rest still rank.
