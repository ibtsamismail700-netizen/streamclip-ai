Keyword Finder

Find what to make next. Real Google search suggestions are collected for your seed, then your key clusters them and estimates intent and difficulty — labelled as estimates, because that is what they are.

Step 1 — Your seed

How many keywords

How this tool works

1
Type a seed keyword — the broader subject you make content or write about.
2
Pick the niche, country and language, and how many keywords you want.
3
Tap Find keywords — real Google autosuggest suggestions are fetched for your seed and its question variants (no key needed for that part), then Gemini clusters and estimates them.
4
Use the list: copy keywords, or tap Use in a title → on any row to open the Hooks + Title Generator with that keyword already filled in.

What you need

  • Your free Gemini key added in Settings (one minute, free)
  • One seed keyword

What you get

  • 10–30 related keywords with intent (Informational / Commercial)
  • A difficulty estimate (Low / Medium / High) and a relative demand estimate per keyword
  • A one-line content angle per keyword
  • A "Use in a title" handoff into the Hooks tool

Tips

  • Long, specific keywords (Low difficulty) are where small channels and sites actually win.
  • The demand number compares keywords inside your list only — never read it as real search volume.
  • Run the same seed in two languages — Urdu and English lists often reveal completely different openings.

FAQ

Are the search volumes real?

No — and the page says so on the results. There is no real volume database behind a free key. The suggestions are real (straight from Google autosuggest); the difficulty and demand are AI estimates for comparing keywords against each other.

The suggestions step said the endpoint did not answer.

Google's public suggest endpoint sometimes refuses. The finder then generates from the seed alone and tells you it did — the estimates still work.

What do I do with a keyword?

Make the video or article it names. "Use in a title" sends it to the Hooks tool; the Blog Writer turns it into an article.
