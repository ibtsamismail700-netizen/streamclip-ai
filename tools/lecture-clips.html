Lecture to Revision Clips

Student Mode: turn a long lecture into short revision clips of the key concepts — upload the lecture or let the AI map the concepts from the transcript.

Option A — upload the lecture video

Each concept becomes one 30–60 second revision clip. Export one concept at a time.

Option B — let AI map the concepts first

Paste the lecture transcript; the AI lists the key concepts in teaching order, with memory hooks.

How this tool works

1
Fastest path: paste the lecture transcript and tap "Find key concepts" — you get the concept map first.
2
Upload the lecture video and use the concept timestamps to set the Start and End sliders.
3
Play the concept to check it starts and ends cleanly.
4
Export it as a revision clip (WebM), then repeat for the next concept.
5
Revise by watching your own 1-minute clips the night before the exam.

What you need

  • Your free Gemini key added in Settings (one minute, free)
  • The lecture video file, or its transcript for the concept map

What you get

  • A concept-by-concept revision plan with memory hooks
  • One short exported clip per concept

Tips

  • Name each exported clip with the concept ("Clip 3 — Supply and Demand") so revision stays organised.
  • Keep clips under 60 seconds — one concept per clip.
  • Make the clips the same day you attend the lecture, while it is fresh.

FAQ

Can it take a YouTube lecture link directly?

Browsers cannot download YouTube videos. Upload the lecture file, or paste the transcript (YouTube → "…" → "Open transcript") for the concept map and cut from your own copy.

How long should a revision clip be?

30–60 seconds: one concept, one example, one hook line.
