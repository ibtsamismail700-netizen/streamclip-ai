Caption Translation

Translate captions or subtitle text into another language while keeping every line break and timestamp exactly in place.

Translate your captions

How this tool works

1
Paste your captions — SRT format or one caption per line both work.
2
Choose the target language.
3
Tap "Translate captions". Only the spoken words are translated; timestamps and line breaks stay untouched.
4
Copy the result and paste it into your editor or upload it as a subtitle file.

What you need

  • Your free Gemini key added in Settings (one minute, free)
  • Your caption text

What you get

  • A full translation in the same format, ready to paste into any editor
  • One-tap copy button

Tips

  • Translate one language at a time for the cleanest result.
  • Short caption lines translate better than long paragraphs.
  • Check names and game terms after translation — AI can localise them.

FAQ

Does it keep my timestamps?

Yes. The translator is instructed to keep every timestamp and line break exactly as pasted.

Which languages are supported?

The listed languages plus most languages Gemini knows — pick the closest option and name your language at the top of the caption box if needed.
