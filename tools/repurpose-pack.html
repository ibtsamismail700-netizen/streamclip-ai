Repurpose Pack

Stop re-doing the same video three times. One link becomes the complete TikTok + Shorts + Reels kit in a single job.

Step 1 — The video

Step 2 — Pack options

Captions are generated by listening to the video, then translated with every timestamp kept. Leave languages 2 and 3 on None if you only need one.

How this tool works

1
Paste the YouTube link of the video you want to spread across platforms.
2
Pick the tone and up to three caption languages.
3
Tap Make the repurpose pack — the progress ring walks through watching, moment-picking, writing, captioning and translating.
4
Copy each platform's kit with its own button, download the .srt captions, and note the three clip moments.
5
Cut the moments in the Stream Clipper using the same link and the listed times.

What you need

  • Your free Gemini key added in Settings (one minute, free)
  • One YouTube video link

What you get

  • 3 titles, a description and hashtags for each of TikTok, Shorts and Reels
  • Captions (.srt) in the original language plus up to 3 translations
  • The 3 best clip moments with scores and Watch links
  • Best posting-time guidance per platform

Tips

  • The posting times are general guidance from common creator practice — your own analytics always beat any chart.
  • Read AI captions once before posting: names and numbers can be misheard.
  • This is a long job on a free key (scan + writing + captions). The progress ring shows exactly where it is.

FAQ

Why do captions stop at 30 minutes?

Caption generation listens to the video end to end; 30 minutes keeps the job reliable on a free key. It is stated on the pack whenever a video is longer.

Are the titles invented?

They are written from what the video actually is — the writer is instructed never to promise content the video does not have.

Can I get more clip moments?

Yes — the Stream Clipper scans the same link with full controls (length, count, score). This pack surfaces the top 3 as part of the kit.
