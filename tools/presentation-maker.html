How this tool works

1
Optionally add the source: the YouTube lecture or PDF this presentation is based on — or your professor's reference PDF, whose topics are listed on the page before anything is generated.
2
Write your topic and any points your teacher said must be covered. With a reference PDF, your topic leads as the subject and the slides follow the PDF's topics and structure.
3
Choose the number of slides and your level, then tap "Plan my presentation" — you get a title, a slide-by-slide plan with bullets, and the exact words to say on each slide.
4
Build the slides in PowerPoint/Google Slides using the bullets, then record yourself reading the voice script — or press Play on any slide to hear the AI voice read it.
5
Download the real files: a PPT (.pptx) with your narration in each slide's speaker notes, a PDF with one slide per page, or the voice script as a text file. "Play all" reads the whole deck aloud slide by slide.

Tips

  • Practice the script once out loud — it is written to sound natural, not read.
  • Keep slides visual: bullets on the slide, full sentences in your voice.
  • Time yourself: each slide's script is roughly 20–30 seconds.

FAQ

Does it make the video itself?

It makes the full plan and voice script — the two hard parts. Recording is you reading the script over your slides, which most teachers prefer anyway because the voice is yours.

What does the reference PDF change?

Everything about the content: the AI reads it first, shows you its topic list, then plans the slides along those topics — your typed topic still leads as the subject.

Can I change a slide?

Regenerate with the extra point added in the points box, or edit the downloaded script directly.
