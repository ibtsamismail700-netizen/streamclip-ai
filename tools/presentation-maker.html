How this tool works

1
Write your topic and any points your teacher said must be covered.
2
Choose the number of slides and your level.
3
Tap "Plan my presentation" — you get a title, a slide-by-slide plan with bullets, and the exact words to say on each slide.
4
Build the slides in PowerPoint/Google Slides using the bullets, then record yourself reading the voice script — or read it slide by slide over a screen recording.
5
Download the voice script as a text file for practice.

What you need

  • Your free Gemini key added in Settings (one minute, free)
  • Your topic (and your teacher's must-cover points, if any)

What you get

  • A complete slide plan with bullets
  • A ready-to-read narration script for every slide, downloadable as .txt
  • One-tap AI voice playback and .wav download per slide, in your chosen Gemini voice and language

Tips

  • Practice the script once out loud — it is written to sound natural, not read.
  • Keep slides visual: bullets on the slide, full sentences in your voice.
  • Time yourself: each slide's script is roughly 20–30 seconds.

FAQ

Does it make the video itself?

It makes the full plan and voice script — the two hard parts. Recording is you reading the script over your slides, which most teachers prefer anyway because the voice is yours.

Can I change a slide?

Regenerate with the extra point added in the points box, or edit the downloaded script directly.
