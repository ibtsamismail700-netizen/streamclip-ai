Score My Short

A strict coach for one short: four grades, one overall score, and fixes in the order you should make them — before the algorithm judges it for you.

Step 1 — Your short

Step 2 — Judging context

How this tool works

1
Paste the YouTube link of your short, or upload the video file itself.
2
Tell it the platform and category so the grading is fair.
3
Tap Score my short — Gemini watches the whole video and grades Hook, Pacing, Visual and Title out of 10.
4
Read the report: what is working, then "Fix these — in this order", starting at the top.
5
Copy the rewritten title and the stronger first-3-seconds hook, fix the video, and score it again.

What you need

  • Your free Gemini key added in Settings (one minute, free)
  • A YouTube link to your short, or the video file (up to ~18 MB)

What you get

  • Hook / Pacing / Visual / Title grades with score bars
  • An overall score out of 100
  • A specific, ordered fix list — no vague advice
  • A rewritten title and opening hook, plus a shareable verdict line

Tips

  • Score BEFORE posting, not after — the fixes are cheap while the video is still in your editor.
  • Most shorts honestly grade 3–7 per item. A 9 or 10 is rare and means it is genuinely strong.
  • Fix one round, re-score, and watch which grade moved — that is how you learn what your audience rewards.

FAQ

Is the grading fair?

It is strict on purpose and based only on what is in the video — the coach never invents scenes. Treat it as a strong second opinion, not a guarantee of views.

Why does Title get graded on an upload?

With no platform title to read, the opening frame and on-screen text are graded as the promise the viewer clicked for.

Can it score a long video?

It is built for shorts. Long videos can be graded, but pacing expectations differ — the Stream Clipper and Podcast Clipper serve long content better.
