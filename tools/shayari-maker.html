Shayari / Poetry Video Maker

Pick a mood and a language — get original verses written for you, a moody AI background for each, and every verse spoken in a deep voice. Everything a poetry video is made of.

How this tool works

1
Pick the mood, language, voice and verse count.
2
Tap Make my shayari pack — the verses are written first, as one flowing set.
3
Each verse then gets its own AI background painting, and is voiced in your chosen voice — play it right on the card.
4
Download the backgrounds and voices verse by verse, or the whole pack as a file, and assemble them in the render step or any editor.

What you need

  • Your free Gemini key added in Settings (one minute, free)
  • Nothing else — a mood is enough

What you get

  • Original verses (2, 4 or 6) in Urdu, Hindi or Roman
  • One vertical AI background per verse, with the verse previewed over it
  • One downloadable voice recording (.wav) per verse
  • The full pack as a .md file

Tips

  • Sad and Sufi moods travel furthest on poetry pages — Attitude works best with a strong delivery voice.
  • Post verses as a series (Part 1, Part 2…) — arcs make people follow for the rest.
  • If one background misses the mood, regenerate just by running again — verses are rewritten fresh each run.

FAQ

Is the poetry copied from famous poets?

No — the writer is instructed to create original verses and avoid famous lines. The style is classic; the words are new.

Does it make the finished video?

It makes everything the video is assembled from — backgrounds, words and voices. The assembly itself is the render step, and the page says so rather than faking an MP4.

Can I request a topic inside the mood?

The mood sets the theme. For a personal verse (a name, a city), run it, then ask in the pack notes — a future version may add topics; today the mood is the input.
