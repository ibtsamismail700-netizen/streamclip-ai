Urdu/Hindi Dub Maker

One video, a new language: timed lines extracted, translated the way dub artists speak, and voiced line by line — ready for the render step or any editor.

Step 1 — The video

Step 2 — Dub options

How this tool works

1
Paste a YouTube link or upload the video file, then tap Extract the spoken lines. You get the timed line list in the original language — check it first.
2
Choose the target language, the dub style (natural dub or narrator), and one of the 30 AI voices.
3
Tap Generate the dub — the lines are translated in order, then every line is voiced. The progress ring shows translation, then voicing line by line.
4
Play or download each line's audio, and download the full dubbed script as a timed .srt in the target language plus the two-language lines sheet (.md).
5
Give the pack to the render step (or any video editor) to lay the dub over the video at the listed times.

What you need

  • Your free Gemini key added in Settings (one minute, free)
  • A YouTube link, or a video file with speech (up to ~18 MB)

What you get

  • Every spoken line translated and voiced, with play + download per line
  • A timed .srt of the full dub in the target language
  • A lines sheet (.md) with original and dub side by side

Tips

  • A dub pack is capped at 60 lines per run and YouTube extraction at the first 30 minutes — both limits are stated on the page and on the results.
  • Natural dub aims for similar line lengths so the voice roughly fits the original seconds; exact lip-sync is a render-step craft.
  • Voicing is the longest step on a free key — the ring counts lines as they land, and a failed line keeps its text with the reason shown.

FAQ

Why is the dub not already on the video?

Mixing audio onto the video file is the render step, which this website does not run yet. What you get today is the complete timed dub pack — script, .srt and every line's audio — ready for that step, and the page says so plainly.

Natural dub or Narrator — which should I pick?

Natural dub for dramas, interviews and anything with real speakers: meaning and emotion, in spoken phrasing. Narrator for explainers and documentaries, where one retelling voice sounds better than many.

Can I dub into other languages?

This maker is built for Urdu, Hindi, Roman Urdu, Roman Hindi and Bangla — the languages its audience asked for. Caption Translation covers 100+ languages for subtitles.
