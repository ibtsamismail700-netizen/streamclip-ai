How this tool works

1
Tap "Choose a video file" and pick a recording from your device.
2
The video preview opens — use the Start and End sliders to choose your clip (minimum 3 seconds).
3
Tap "Play this part" to check it plays exactly the piece you want.
4
Tap "Export clip (WebM)" and keep the tab open while it records in real time; the file downloads automatically.
5
Optional: paste the transcript and let the AI rank the best moments before you trim.

What you need

  • Any video file on your device (MP4/WebM work best)
  • For the AI moment finder: the video transcript

What you get

  • A trimmed clip exported as WebM, made entirely in your browser
  • An AI-ranked list of best moments with hooks

Tips

  • Trim tight: start a second before the action, end right after the payoff.
  • Export at the quality you uploaded — the browser records what it plays.
  • For vertical Shorts, record/crop your source vertically where possible.

FAQ

Why WebM and not MP4?

The browser's built-in recorder produces WebM. YouTube, TikTok and most editors accept WebM directly. Server-side MP4 rendering needs an FFmpeg server, which this free website does not run.

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. The file never leaves your device — preview, trimming and export all happen in your browser.
