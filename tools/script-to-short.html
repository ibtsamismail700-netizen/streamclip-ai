Script-to-Short

One topic in — a complete short out: script, scene pictures, voice track and posting text. Everything the render step needs, in one pack.

Step 1 — Topic or script

Step 2 — Style of the short

Target duration
Sets the script length and the number of scenes.

How this tool works

1
Write a topic (or paste your full script and choose "use it as-is"). In Script Studio mode, the first tap writes 5 hooks instead — tap the one your short should open with, then tap again and the full script is built from that exact line.
2
Pick the niche, language, target duration, visual style and voice.
3
Tap Make my short pack — the script is written first, scene by scene, with a scroll-stopping hook as line one.
4
Each scene then gets its own AI picture, and the whole script is voiced in your chosen voice — play it or download the .wav.
5
Copy the posting pack (title, caption, hashtags) or download the full pack as a file.

What you need

  • Your free Gemini key added in Settings (one minute, free)
  • A topic in a sentence — or a finished script of your own

What you get

  • A scene-by-scene script with a hook first line
  • One downloadable AI picture per scene
  • The full script as a downloadable AI voice track (.wav)
  • Posting title, caption and hashtags, ready to paste

Tips

  • Specific topics beat broad ones: "3 habits that quietly waste money" outperforms "money tips".
  • Image generation is the slowest step on a free key — the progress ring shows each scene as it lands.
  • If one scene picture fails, its real error is shown on that card and the rest of the pack still completes.

FAQ

Is the finished video in the pack?

The pack holds everything a video is assembled from — script, pictures, voice — but the assembly itself is the render step, which this website does not run yet. Nothing here fakes an MP4.

Will it rewrite my script?

Only if you choose "A topic". In "My full script" mode your words are used exactly as written — the AI only splits them into scenes and describes the pictures.

Why 30 voices?

They are Gemini's built-in voices, the same set as the Presentation Maker — pick a different one and regenerate if the first does not fit your niche.
