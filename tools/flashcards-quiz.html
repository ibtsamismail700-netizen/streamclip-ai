How this tool works

1
Add your source — a YouTube lecture link, a PDF, or pasted text — and tap Analyse once.
2
Choose your deck size, quiz size and difficulty, then tap the button: the AI builds them from your material only.
3
Tap a flashcard to flip it; use Previous/Next to move through the deck.
4
Answer the quiz by tapping an option — right and wrong answers are marked instantly, with the reason.

What you need

  • Your free Gemini key added in Settings (one minute, free)
  • A YouTube lecture, a PDF (up to ~18 MB), or your study text

What you get

  • A tap-to-flip flashcard deck
  • An MCQ quiz with instant checking, explanations and a score

Tips

  • Do the flashcards first, then the quiz — in that order.
  • Regenerate with a different chapter for a fresh deck.
  • Say flashcard answers out loud before flipping; active recall beats reading.

FAQ

Are the questions from my material or from general knowledge?

From your material only — the generator is instructed to stay exact to the lecture or PDF you analysed.

Can I save the deck?

Download it as a text file from the deck, or just regenerate — it takes seconds with the same analysed source.
