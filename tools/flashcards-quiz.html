Flashcards + Quiz

Student Mode: paste any study text and get tap-to-flip flashcards plus a self-checking MCQ quiz, made from your material only.

Your study material

How this tool works

1
Paste your study text — notes, a chapter, or a lecture transcript.
2
Tap the button: the AI builds 8 flashcards and a 6-question quiz from your text only.
3
Tap a flashcard to flip it; use Previous/Next to move through the deck.
4
Answer the quiz by tapping an option — right and wrong answers are marked instantly, with the reason.

What you need

  • Your free Gemini key added in Settings (one minute, free)
  • Your study text (at least a few paragraphs)

What you get

  • A tap-to-flip flashcard deck
  • A 6-question MCQ quiz with instant checking and explanations

Tips

  • Do the flashcards first, then the quiz — in that order.
  • Regenerate with a different chapter section for a fresh deck.
  • Say flashcard answers out loud before flipping; active recall beats reading.

FAQ

Are the questions from my text or from general knowledge?

From your text only — the generator is instructed to stay exact to what you pasted.

Can I save the deck?

Regenerating takes seconds with the same text, so most students just rebuild it. Your text is not stored on this site.
