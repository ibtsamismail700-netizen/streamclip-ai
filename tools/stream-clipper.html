How this tool works

1
For YouTube: paste the stream link — Gemini watches the video itself on Google's side, in 10-minute windows. For Twitch or private videos: use the Transcript tab and paste the transcript.
2
Choose the stream type and clip style so the AI scores the right kind of moments.
3
Tap "Find best moments" — moments appear live as each part is watched/read, each scored 0–99 with a title, hook and reason.
4
The best moments (above your minimum score) are pre-picked for posting. Change any pick with one tap, then copy the picked list.
5
Cut those timestamps from the stream video (or upload the recording in Upload Your Own Video) and post.

What you need

  • Your free Gemini key added in Settings (one minute, free)
  • A public YouTube stream link — or a transcript for Twitch/private videos

What you get

  • A ranked list of the best moments with viral scores
  • A title and hook for every moment
  • Exact timestamps to cut, best moments pre-picked

Tips

  • Match the stream type honestly — a podcast scored like a gaming stream gives odd picks.
  • Pick moments that make sense to someone who never watched the stream.
  • Post the strongest moment first while you cut the rest.

FAQ

Can the site download the video from my link and cut it?

No — downloading from YouTube/Twitch needs a server-side downloader engine, which a browser-only website cannot run. What changed: Gemini now watches the YouTube video itself from the link to find the moments. Cutting the file itself is done in Upload Your Own Video.

What makes a moment score high?

Big reactions, clutch plays, fails, arguments, reveals and punchlines — moments that stand alone without context.
