Stream Clipper

Paste a stream transcript and let AI pick the viral moments — with scores, hooks and titles for each clip.

One honest note first.
A website in a browser cannot download a video from YouTube or Twitch — that needed the phone app's built-in downloader, which is not part of this website. So here you work from the stream's transcript (on YouTube: open the video → "…" → "Open transcript" → copy). The AI reads it and picks the exact moments to clip, with timestamps, hooks and titles. To cut the video itself, use Upload Your Own Video.

How this tool works

1
Copy the transcript of the stream from YouTube (video page → "…" → "Open transcript").
2
Paste the stream link above for reference, then paste the transcript.
3
Tap "Find best moments" — the AI reads the whole transcript and scores each strong moment 0–99.
4
You get a ranked list: timestamp, catchy title, hook line and why the moment works.
5
Cut those timestamps from the stream video (or upload the recording in Upload Your Own Video) and post.

What you need

  • Your free Gemini key added in Settings (one minute, free)
  • The stream transcript (timestamps help the AI give exact cut points)

What you get

  • A ranked list of the best moments with viral scores
  • A title and hook for every moment
  • Exact timestamps to cut

Tips

  • Longer transcripts find better moments — a full stream beats a 10-minute piece.
  • Pick moments that make sense to someone who never watched the stream.
  • Post the strongest moment first while you cut the rest.

FAQ

Why can the site not just take my link and cut the video?

Downloading from YouTube/Twitch needs a server-side downloader engine. A browser-only website cannot do it, so this tool works from the transcript and the Upload tool cuts the actual video file.

What makes a moment score high?

Big reactions, clutch plays, fails, arguments, reveals and punchlines — moments that stand alone without context.
