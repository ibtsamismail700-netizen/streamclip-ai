Dukaan Promo Maker

Made for small shops and dukaan owners: type your shop name and offers once, and get the full promo pack — poster, WhatsApp message, status voice and caption.

Step 1 — Your shop

Step 2 — Language, tone & voice

How this tool works

1
Write the shop name, type, and your products/offers — one per line, prices exactly as you sell them.
2
Pick the language and tone, and the voice for the status script.
3
Tap Make my promo pack — first the words are written (broadcast message, caption, script), then the poster is designed, then the script is voiced.
4
Post the poster, send the broadcast message to your WhatsApp list, and put the voice over any product clip — or over the poster itself — as your status.

What you need

  • Your free Gemini key added in Settings (one minute, free)
  • Your shop name and your real offers, with real prices

What you get

  • An AI poster carrying your strongest offer as its headline
  • A ready WhatsApp broadcast message
  • A 20–30 second status-video script + the AI voice recording (.wav)
  • A posting caption with hashtags

Tips

  • One strong offer beats five weak ones — put your best deal first in the list.
  • Check the poster's words letter by letter before posting; if the AI misspells a word, run the maker again.
  • Send the broadcast at the hour your customers actually order — for food, late afternoon works.

FAQ

Will it invent discounts to make the promo sound better?

No — the writer is instructed to use only the prices and offers you typed, exactly as written.

Can I use the poster on Facebook and Instagram too?

Yes — it is a 4:5 poster, the size both platforms favour for feed posts.

The voice is in the wrong language style.

The voice reads the script in your chosen language. If a word sounds off, fix the spelling in the offers list (Roman spellings read best) and run again.
