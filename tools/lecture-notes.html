Lecture to Notes

Student Mode: paste a lecture transcript and get clean, structured, downloadable notes — summary, key points, definitions and formulas.

Step 1 — The lecture text

Get the transcript from the lecture video (YouTube → "…" → "Open transcript") or from your class recording's captions, and paste it here.

Step 2 — Note style

How this tool works

1
Copy the lecture transcript and paste it in the box.
2
Tap "Make my notes" — the AI reads the whole lecture once.
3
You get a titled note set: summary, key points, definitions, formulas & facts, and a "remember" list.
4
Download the notes as a Markdown file, or copy them into your notebook app.

What you need

  • Your free Gemini key added in Settings (one minute, free)
  • The lecture transcript (the longer and cleaner, the better the notes)

What you get

  • Structured, downloadable revision notes
  • Definitions and formulas pulled out separately

Tips

  • Clean transcripts make clean notes — remove "[Music]" and applause lines first.
  • Make notes per lecture, not per course — small sets revise better.
  • Read the "remember" list out loud once; it is built for last-minute revision.

FAQ

Will it invent facts not in the lecture?

The notes writer is instructed to stay exact to your transcript. Still, glance through before an exam — you are responsible for your final notes.

What format is the download?

Markdown (.md) — it opens in Notion, Obsidian, Google Docs (via import) and any text editor.
