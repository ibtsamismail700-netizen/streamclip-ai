How StreamClip AI works

1
Add your Gemini key once. Follow the step-by-step guide on the Settings page — it takes about a minute and costs nothing.
2
Pick a tool. Clipping, thumbnails, captions, hooks — or Student Mode for lectures, notes, flashcards and presentations.
3
The AI works in your browser. Your key talks directly to Google Gemini. Your files and text are not stored on this site.
4
Download and post. Copy the result, export the clip, or download your notes — then publish on Shorts, TikTok or Reels.

Honest limits

Browsers cannot download videos directly from YouTube or Twitch, and this site has no server rendering engine — so for streams you work from the transcript (free from YouTube's "Open transcript"), and uploaded videos are trimmed and exported right in your browser as WebM. Nothing here fakes a result: if a step needs a server engine, the tool page says so plainly.

FAQ

Is StreamClip AI really free?

Yes. The site is free; the AI runs on your own free Gemini key, which has a generous free limit from Google.

Where does my Gemini key go?

Only into your browser's local storage and, when a tool runs, directly to Google's Gemini API. It is never sent to this site or anyone else.

Can I clip any stream?

Only clip content you own or have permission to use, and follow the platform's rules and the creator's rights.
