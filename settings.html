How to create your Gemini key — step by step

1
Open Google AI Studio in your browser: go to aistudio.google.com.
2
Sign in with any Google (Gmail) account — the same account you use for YouTube is fine.
3
Open the API key page: click "Get API key" in the left menu (or go to aistudio.google.com/apikey).
4
Click "Create API key". If it asks, choose "Create API key in new project".
5
Google creates your key — a long code starting with AIza. Click the copy icon next to it.
6
Come back to this page and paste the key into the box above.
7
Tap "Check and add". Your key is saved in your browser right away, and StreamClip AI then checks it with one tiny call to Google and shows you the result.
8
Done. Every AI tool on this site is now unlocked. You can add up to 10 keys — keys from two different Google accounts give you double the free limit.

The key is free. Google's free limit is large enough for daily clipping and study use. Never share your key with anyone — treat it like a password. If a key ever leaks, delete it in AI Studio and create a new one.
