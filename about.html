About StreamClip AI

StreamClip AI is a free set of browser tools for two kinds of people: creators who turn long streams and videos into Shorts, and students who turn long lectures into revision clips, notes, flashcards and presentations.

The idea is simple — AI listens, finds the moments that matter, and writes the hooks and captions — built for the web as a full toolkit: a stream clipper, a thumbnail creator/redesigner, caption translation, a hooks and title generator, your-own-branding, and a full Student Mode with lecture notes, flashcards, exam questions and a lecture tutor that answers only from your own material.

There is no account, no subscription and no AI bill for anyone: every tool runs on the visitor's own free Gemini key from Google AI Studio, sent from their browser directly to Google. Files you upload are processed on your own device and are not stored by this site.

Honesty note: a browser cannot download videos from YouTube or Twitch, and this site runs no server rendering engine — the pages say plainly where a transcript or an in-browser export is used instead. We would rather label a limit than fake a result.
