How to add your free Gemini key

Every AI tool on StreamClip AI runs on your own free Gemini key from Google AI Studio. The site has no key of its own and no AI bill — that is what keeps the tools free. Adding your key takes about a minute:

The 8 steps

1
Open Google AI Studio in your browser: go to aistudio.google.com.
2
Sign in with any Google (Gmail) account — the same account you use for YouTube is fine.
3
Open the API key page: click "Get API key" in the left menu (or go to aistudio.google.com/apikey).
4
Click "Create API key". If it asks, choose "Create API key in new project".
5
Google creates your key — a long code starting with AIza. Click the copy icon next to it.
6
Come back to the Settings page and paste the key into the box.
7
Tap "Add key". Your key is saved in your browser right away — no check, no waiting.
8
Done. Every AI tool on this site is now unlocked.

Where your key lives

Your key is stored only in your own browser, on your own device. When a tool runs, your browser sends the key directly to Google's Gemini API — it never passes through this site, and the site's operator never sees it. You can remove it any time in Settings, or clear everything the site keeps with "Clear all StreamClip AI data" on the same page.

One key or several?

One key is enough to start. Each free key has its own separate limits, and StreamClip AI rotates between your keys automatically — so adding a second or third key (from a different Google account) makes "busy" messages rare. If you ever wonder whether a key works, the Test my key button in Settings makes one tiny real call per kind — text, image and voice — and shows Working, Resting or the real error.

Key safety, in one breath

  • Your Gemini key is a password for your free quota — do not post it publicly or send it to strangers.
  • Only paste it into sites you trust; on StreamClip AI it stays in your browser.
  • If a key ever leaks, delete it in Google AI Studio and create a fresh one — it takes a minute.

FAQ

Is the Gemini key really free?

Yes — Google AI Studio gives every Google account a free tier for the Gemini API. Free limits apply (see our guide to free limits), but for everyday creator tools they go a long way.

Is my key safe here?

Your key is saved only in your browser and sent only to Google when a tool runs. It is never sent to this site. You can delete it from Settings at any time.

The tool says my key was not accepted — what now?

Copy the key again from AI Studio (no spaces at the ends), or create a fresh key and add that instead. The Settings page's Test my key button shows Google's real answer.
