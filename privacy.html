Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

What this site stores

StreamClip AI has no user accounts and no database of visitors. Everything below is saved only in your own browser (localStorage), on your device:

  • your Gemini key(s) — the only thing the tools cannot work without;
  • the list of Gemini models your key can use (cached so tools start fast);
  • Your Shorts — small poster pictures of clips you exported (the video files themselves were downloaded to your device; they are not kept);
  • Your Work — the results the tools generate (hook packs, translations, notes, decks, scan results, thumbnail versions), so they survive a refresh;
  • your branding kit (name, logo, colours) if you use the Branding tool;
  • your current lecture source for the student tools — including the text extracted from a PDF you analysed, so the other student tools can use it without re-reading the file;
  • small working notes: which notes-sheet design comes next, a one-time handoff when you tap a live stream into the Clipper, drafts of the contact form, and an activity log of the last 20 tool errors (kept so you can see and share exactly what went wrong).

Separately, your browser's sessionStorage holds a short-lived note of which of your key's models are resting after a rate-limit hit; it clears itself when the browser session ends. You can remove everything at any time with "Clear all StreamClip AI data" in Settings, or by clearing your browser data.

Your Gemini key

When you run an AI tool, your key and the text or image you submitted are sent from your browser directly to Google's Gemini API (generativelanguage.googleapis.com). They are never sent to this site's operator. Google's handling of API data is covered by Google's own privacy and API terms.

Your files

Videos and images you open in the tools are processed on your device. They are not uploaded to this site.

Cookies and advertising

This site may show ads served by Google AdSense in the future. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings (adssettings.google.com) and at aboutads.info. This site itself sets no tracking cookies of its own.

Contact form

The Contact page has no server behind it: when you write a message there, it is saved only in your own browser, on this device. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else, and we cannot read it. The page says this above the form as well.

Children

This site is a general-audience tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
